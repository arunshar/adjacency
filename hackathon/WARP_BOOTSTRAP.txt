a typed, hashed PolicySpec, inventory is evaluated, and every verdict passes through
deterministic fail-closed gates G0 to G6. ImagineSignal extends the same discipline to

declared visual attribute changes and everything else stays locked and byte-verified,
with gates IS0 to IS8 and an append-once decision receipt. The output is not an image,
it is a decision: keep, edit, test, hold, review, or stop, with the evidence class that
justified it attached.
